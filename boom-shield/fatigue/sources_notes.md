# Source notes (search-result summaries only; all UNVERIFIED, no page fetched)
S1 PE-100 / PE4710 pipe S-N (ETASR 422 Bourchak/Aid; eXPRESS Polym Lett 2017; UTA Esfahani dissertation): S-N curves exist, values not in snippets.
S2 Gelbo flex ASTM F392: 440 deg twist + compression; condition A 2700 cycles (1 h) .. E 20 cycles; >50 pinholes/300 cm2 "significant" (vendor blog); patent: PE-only worse than PP-containing at 38 F; one-hour Gelbo 45 pinholes on a comparative barrier film. (intertek, ametek, USPTO 9701445/12722878)
S3 Stratospheric airship laminates (Kang et al. 2006 KAIST; JAXA 2004 flight-test residual strength; Frontiers Mater 2022 Lv et al.): Vectran laminates usable -75..65 C; flown samples lost strength to UV/ozone/particles; no fold-cycle numbers in snippets.
S4 TARS: envelope life 5-6 yr (Marfa Public Radio), replacement ~$4M; PU-coated Tedlar fabric; Frontiers 2022: PVF/PVDF best weathering layers; one material 82 -> 15 kgf/cm after 24 months coastal ageing.
S5 ETFE cushions: manufacturers estimate foil life >= 50 yr (2011 conf. paper, scipedia); creep under constant tension is main long-term concern (Waterloo thesis); Munich cushion roof (2011) foils intact; DFG project on cyclic multiaxial ETFE (gepris 527832586).
S6 PTFE/glass: Hajj Terminal (1981) original membrane in place after 29 yr (Birdair 2010, promotional); DFG project 557719891: cold-installation creases -> small cracks that grow, premature replacement; supplier life 25-30 yr, some >45-50 yr.
S7 UHMWPE: Dyneema SK78 ">=3x fatigue of generic HMPE" (vendor); Delaware posters: UHMWPE composite panels 250k-500k cycles -> fibre strength -20..-36 %; old patent flex-over-wire fit N = 4.5e7 - (stress g/d)^4.2 (USPTO 4584347).
S8 Polymer endurance limits: PE, PP, PTFE, PMMA, epoxy show an endurance limit at 1e7 cycles; nylon, PET do not (engineerexcel summary; PMC11207950).
S9 Balloon PE at cold: equatorial tropopause ~-85 C makes balloon plastics brittle (DRDO Def Sci J); 1964 NCAR Panama: StratoFilm less brittle than standard PE (stratocat CLY-19640808/09/13); 1969 NCAR impact toughness at +23 and -80 C: brittle at -80 C (UCAR dataset); NASA CR 1989 SF-85 tests at 23/-60/-90/-120 C, notch sensitivity (JAXA repo / NTRS 20040079382); at 190 K PE stiffness and yield rise dramatically (NASA Goddard).
S10 Sails: flogging/flex = top life-limiter; carbon/aramid poor in repeated bending; laminates lose strength when repeatedly creased; woven polyester best in flex (North Sails 2018 "Four F's"; Ocean Navigator; Sail Mag; boats.com) - qualitative.
S11 Vectran (Kuraray, vendor): coated fabric crease x100 -> 0.8 % tenacity loss vs 22.9 % aramid; ASTM D2176 yarn: Vectran 115k cycles vs aramid 1.4-15k.
S12 UV: PET/PA/PET balloon film under stratosphere-like UV -> -50 % stress/strain at break (Lewandowski thesis, Toulouse / HAL 01467061); Kondyurin et al 2013 PE at 40 km (arXiv 1109.5457), LDEF balloon films (NTRS 19910015707) - numbers not in snippets.
S13 ULDB super-pressure PE balloon (NASA): 100-day goal, 33.5 km; 2008-09 Antarctic test 11 days, failed to regain pressure; failures so far "not allowing extended durations" (Cathey & Pierce); seal peeled at ~55 Pa (stratocat). Mylar SPB: random pinholes (NTRS 19670009435).
S14 Elastomers: NR poor ozone resistance; silicone ozone-resistant, rated to ~-50 C (generic tables); no -60 C fatigue data found.
S15 PET film S-N at 1e7: not found (only Ag/PET bending fatigue, KAIST).
S16 Superpressure balloons: neutral oscillation 2-3 min, Brunt-Vaisala ~4 min; SPBs depart isopycnic for periods < 15-20 min (Vincent & Hertzog 2014; Podglajen SPARC 2016); mountain waves linked to large balloon excursions.
[K] = handbook-class values from the author's background knowledge, not from these searches.
