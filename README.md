# Skyloop  
  
![Skyloop pod and tube](docs/imgs/skyloop-pod-tube.png)  
  
Skyloop is a vision for a buoyant, near vacuum sky transport system connecting major cities via stratospheric corridors. By suspending lightweight tubes high above the weather for launching and linearly accelerating small gliders inside them, Skyloop aims to deliver intercity travel at Mach 2–6 speeds with energy efficiency and throughput orders of magnitude better than current aviation.

## Boom Shield  
A side study of a tethered stratospheric hydrogen layer that blocks sonic booms under supersonic flight corridors. See [boom-shield/README.md](boom-shield/README.md) for the studies and [boom-shield/STATUS.md](boom-shield/STATUS.md) for the current status and verdict.  

## Skyloop re-review (October 2026)  
A second look at the stratospheric near-vacuum tube using the BoomShield models. Verdict: the floating tube cannot be held straight enough for Mach 2-6, and safe headway caps capacity; a ground tube is the better bet only if it can be built near $50 M/km (see Hyperloop below). See [skyloop-review/README.md](skyloop-review/README.md).

## Hyperloop (our version)  
An elevated ground tube with compliant-gap maglev pods, mapped with the same models. Verdict: the compliant gap solves alignment and there is no physics veto to ~Mach 6 (breach deceleration caps speed), but mountain tunnels lift the cost to ~$120 M/km against a ~$52 M/km break-even, and it needs a certified ~10 s moving block. Keep it as a watching brief at Mach 3–4. See [hyperloop/README.md](hyperloop/README.md).
